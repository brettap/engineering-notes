## ##Controlled Validation Procedure — Planned

1. Begin with an IoT-to-Management-LAN test against the selected nonessential endpoint.
2. Record client address, destination, protocol and port, expected result and test time before evaluating the result.
3. Test permitted and prohibited destinations from the actual VLAN client while retaining the management connection.
4. Record the observed result and relevant firewall logs. A timeout alone should remain inconclusive without evidence separating policy enforcement from endpoint or service failure.
5. Repeat for IoT-to-Trusted and IoT-to-Entertainment access, then Trusted connectivity and Guest isolation according to the intended policy.
6. Review Entertainment rules against required services and document any remaining coverage gaps.
7. Track any configuration correction as a separate controlled change, then repeat the affected client tests.

Do not disable broad firewall rules merely to prove connectivity. No infrastructure change is authorized by this documentation procedure.

**Next Logical Step:** Execute the planned client tests and record correlated firewall evidence, because unexecuted procedures do not establish enforcement.
