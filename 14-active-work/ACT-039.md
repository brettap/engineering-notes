## Issue/Symptoms

Scan to folder stopped working on a new PC; another user’s credentials changed.

## Environment

[organization]; Toshiba MFP; local <username> account and shared folder on new PC.

## Investigation/Troubleshooting

Located scan settings under Registration → Templates → Scans rather than Address Book.

## Root Cause

Stale scan destination/settings on the replacement PC; changed credentials in the second case.

## Resolution

Shared the new PC folder, used a local <username> account, updated Terry’s scan path, and saved both the folder setting and template. Updated <username>’s user/password settings.

## Verification

The source states scanning worked after saving all settings. Individual file names and test counts unknown.

## Lessons/Operational Notes

Both levels of settings had to be saved. The dedicated local scanning identity avoids coupling to email-password changes.
